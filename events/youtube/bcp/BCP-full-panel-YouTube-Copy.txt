Survey Readiness & Immediate Jeopardy | BCP Conference Full Panel

DESCRIPTION
What does survey readiness look like in everyday care—and how should a team respond when serious concerns arise?

Watch the full Best Care Practices conference panel featuring Norman Harris Jr., Cindy Fronning, Jignasa Dabhi and Kelly Shelley. The discussion explores survey preparation, immediate jeopardy, leadership, just culture and keeping teams focused on patients and residents in post-acute and long-term care.

Topics include:
• Building survey readiness into daily practice
• Recognizing red flags and responding as a team
• Supporting staff through difficult situations
• Learning from problems through a just-culture approach
• Community connections and advice for nursing leaders

This full-session recording includes audience questions and discussion.
