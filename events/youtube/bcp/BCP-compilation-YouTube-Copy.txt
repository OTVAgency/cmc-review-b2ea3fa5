TITLE
Care Teams at Work | BCP Conference Attendee Interviews

DESCRIPTION
Meet the people and hear the conversations from the Best Care Practices conference.

Care Teams at Work brings together 14 attendee and sit-down interviews hosted by Norman Harris Jr., including conversations with Shark Bird, Dr. Mark Britton, Brianna Wynne and Diane Sanders-Cepeda.

The interviews explore patient-first care, listening to patients and families, teamwork, professional development, and services supporting post-acute and long-term care teams. Hear attendee and exhibitor perspectives on mobile imaging, palliative care, medication access, podiatry and community connections.

For the panel discussion, watch “Survey Readiness & Immediate Jeopardy | BCP Conference Full Panel.”
