TITLE
Care Teams at Work | BCP Conference Highlights & Interviews

DESCRIPTION
Meet the people, hear the conversations and explore the ideas shared at the Best Care Practices conference.

Care Teams at Work brings together panel highlights and attendee interviews from across the event. From survey readiness and patient-first care to the services supporting post-acute and long-term care teams, this film captures practical perspectives to take back to your workplace.

In this compilation:
• Panel conversations about survey readiness, red flags and immediate jeopardy
• Listening to patients and families
• Attendee and exhibitor perspectives on supporting care teams
• Conversations about mobile imaging, palliative care, medication access and podiatry
• Community connections, professional development and teamwork

Panel highlights feature Norman Harris Jr., Cindy Fronning, Jignasa Dabhi and Kelly Shelley.

For the complete panel conversation, look for “Survey Readiness & Immediate Jeopardy | BCP Conference Full Panel.”
