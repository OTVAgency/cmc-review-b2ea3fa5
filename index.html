About page, main text

Hi, I’m Norman Harris, a licensed nursing home administrator, advocate, and the founder of Comfort Measures Consulting. My passion for elder care began at just 8 years old, volunteering beside my grandmother—an evangelist who brought prayer, comfort, and connection to residents in nursing homes. Her example sparked my lifelong mission: to bring peace, love, and dignity to aging adults and their families.

Today, I guide families and independent healthcare providers through some of life’s most important decisions. Whether you’re feeling overwhelmed by care options or seeking clarity in the chaos, I’m here to offer trusted resources, joyful perspective, and genuine support.

Let’s walk this journey together—one step at a time, with comfort and compassion leading the way.

Comfort Measures Consulting (CMC) helps families, caregivers, and healthcare providers navigate the complexity of aging with clarity and compassion. • Family Navigation & Placement – needs assessment, care planning, vetted options, next steps
